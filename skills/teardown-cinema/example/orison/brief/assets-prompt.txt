1. gore.png, portrait format. A perfectly flat, front-on, evenly lit texture plate of one panel of the creature's dome, filling the whole canvas edge to edge, no perspective, no shadows, no curvature. It is iridescent stained glass lace: hundreds of irregular organic cells of opal glass in teal, rose, pale gold, lilac and pearl, like dragonfly wing or leaded glass, held in fine white-gold veins. Cells are small and dense at the top of the canvas and grow gradually larger toward the bottom. The bottom fifth becomes intricate white-gold filigree lacework. Gaps in the lace are pure black. Left and right edges tile seamlessly with each other.

2. filigree.png, wide landscape format. A perfectly flat, front-on, evenly lit, seamless horizontal band of extremely ornate gold filigree tracery on a pure black background: interlocking gothic circles, arcs, trefoils, small gear teeth and bone-like struts, like the rings of the golden orrery in the reference. Metal only, pure black between the metal. Fills the canvas top to bottom. Left and right edges tile seamlessly.

3. silk.png, wide landscape format. A perfectly flat, front-on, evenly lit, seamless texture filling the whole canvas: pleated iridescent silk or shell, repeating fan-shaped pleats radiating from the bottom edge to the top edge, in pale rose, pearl, teal and champagne gold with a thin bright gold-beaded trim along the top edge. No perspective, no background visible. Left and right edges tile seamlessly.

4. planet.png, square format. The whole gas giant from the reference shown alone, complete disc centred in the frame on a pure black background, with its bands of amber, cream, rust and teal, swirling storms and one large oval storm, lit from the right with a soft terminator falling into shadow on the far left, and a soft atmospheric glow on the lit limb. 5. rings.png, wide landscape format. A flat, straight-on, evenly lit, horizontally uniform cross-section of a planetary ring system, shown as many fine parallel horizontal bands running left to right from edge to edge: creamy white, pale gold and soft grey-lilac bands of different widths and brightness with narrow dark gaps, like a barcode of light rotated to run horizontally. No perspective, no planet, no stars. Top and bottom edges fade to pure black.

6. flyer.png, square format. One small glowing winged creature from the reference seen exactly from above with its wings fully spread and symmetrical, centred on a pure black background: something between a butterfly and a manta ray, with translucent gold and pearl wings, fine luminous veins, a slim glowing body. Pure black around it. No other creatures.
